    Why I Built HustleUPI

    The idea for HustleUPI came from observing how people started paying the same merchant QR code multiple times to split a bill into smaller transactions after the announcement of the new 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on certain merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000. Instead of manually calculating amounts, scanning the same QR repeatedly, and keeping track of which payments were completed, I wanted a simpler experience. HustleUPI solves that hassle. Scan a merchant QR once, enter the bill amount, and HustleUPI intelligently splits the payment into smaller transactions or lets you create your own custom splits. It works alongside your favorite UPI apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM, making the entire payment process faster, cleaner, and easier to manage. This project is my attempt to turn a real-world payment inconvenience into a simple and user-friendly solution.

    Privacy Policy

    Privacy Policy

    Hustle UPI is a client-side static web application. It does not have a backend server, database, or user accounts.

    No data collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, payment credentials, or financial data.

    All QR code scanning, VPA parsing, and payment split calculations happen entirely on your device inside your web browser. When you initiate a payment, Hustle UPI generates a standard upi://pay link and hands it off to your installed UPI app. The app itself never touches your money or processes payments.
